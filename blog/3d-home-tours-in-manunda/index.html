3D Home Tours in Manunda

For investors comparing yield across several Manunda properties at once, a Matterport 3D Showcase does the work of multiple inspections in a fraction of the time.

By The One Club · Published 2026-08-01

Why a 3D tour suits an investor-heavy market

Manunda's buyer base, first-home buyers and yield-focused investors, includes a meaningful share of people comparing several properties in the same price bracket before deciding. A Matterport 3D Showcase lets an investor assess condition, layout and dual-occupancy potential across multiple listings without booking separate inspections for each.

What buyers actually do with it

Investors use the walkthrough to gauge renovation scope and rental appeal quickly, while first-home buyers use it to picture whether a modest post-war home suits their needs before committing a weekend to an in-person visit.

How it works on a Manunda listing

Every One Club listing includes a Matterport 3D Showcase and drone flyover for a flat $199. In Manunda specifically, the drone flyover is genuinely useful for demonstrating block size and any dual-occupancy or secondary-dwelling potential, the exact detail Manunda buyers are comparing against Edge Hill and Whitfield prices next door.

The result for sellers

At Manunda's price point, where competing stock is genuinely comparable, a clear, thorough 3D Showcase is often what separates a listing that gets serious inquiries from one that gets scrolled past.
